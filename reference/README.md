# John Deere 6M — rough proportions

Ballpark figures for a mid-size 6M (e.g. 6155M–6175M). They vary by model and tires, so they're only good enough
for "looks right". Correct them from a spec sheet if you want a specific model.

| Feature | Real (approx.) | 1:10 | 1:12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall length | 4.8 m | 480 mm | 400 mm |
| Wheelbase | 2.6 m | 260 mm | 217 mm |
| Overall width | 2.5 m | 250 mm | 208 mm |
| Height to cab roof | 2.95 m | 295 mm | 246 mm |
| Rear wheel diameter (e.g. 650/65R38) | 1.8 m | 180 mm | 150 mm |
| Front wheel diameter (e.g. 540/65R28) | 1.4 m | 140 mm | 117 mm |

At either scale the body is longer than the 256 mm print bed, so plan to split the hood and chassis.
