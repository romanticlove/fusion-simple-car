Approximate values. Measure your own parts and correct them here if something doesn't fit.

|---|---|---|

| Rear axle | Ø3 steel rod or M3 threaded rod | Print a coupler onto the N20 D-shaft |
| Screws | M2 (Ø2.2 clearance, Ø1.8 self-tap), M3 (Ø3.2 clearance, Ø2.7 self-tap) | Self-tapping into plastic is fine for this project |
| Battery | placeholder box ~60 × 30 × 20 | e.g. 2S Li-ion or 4×AA, measure the real one |
